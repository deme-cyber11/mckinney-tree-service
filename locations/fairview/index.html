Our Services in Fairview

Tree Removal

Fairview's large residential lots often feature towering oaks and pecans that occasionally need to be removed. Whether it is a dead tree posing a risk to your home or a healthy tree that is simply in the wrong location, our removal team works carefully to protect your property throughout the process.

Tree Trimming

Regular trimming keeps Fairview's beautiful tree canopies in top form. We selectively prune to remove deadwood, improve structure, and maintain safe clearance from your home, fence lines, and utility connections while preserving the natural beauty of each tree.

Stump Grinding

A remaining stump can detract from Fairview's immaculate residential landscapes. Our stump grinding service eliminates the stump below grade, allowing you to restore a smooth, usable lawn surface without the hassle of excavation.

Emergency Tree Service

When a severe storm sends a limb crashing through your Fairview property, fast response is essential. Our emergency team prioritizes safety and speed, arriving to remove fallen trees and secure any remaining hazards before additional damage can occur.

Tree Health Assessment

Fairview homeowners often invest significantly in their landscapes. Protect that investment with a professional health assessment that identifies early signs of disease, pest damage, or structural weakness before they become expensive problems.

Lot Clearing

About Fairview

Fairview is a quiet, primarily residential town situated between Allen and McKinney in the heart of Collin County. With a population that has intentionally remained modest, Fairview is defined by its generous one-acre-minimum lot sizes, rolling terrain, and abundant tree cover that gives the community a distinctly rural feel despite its proximity to major suburban centers. Many Fairview properties feature decades-old live oaks, post oaks, and cedar elms that require knowledgeable, careful maintenance to keep healthy and structurally sound. The town's commitment to preserving its open, tree-lined character makes professional tree care an essential service for residents who want to maintain both the safety and beauty of their properties. NTX Tree Experts is honored to serve Fairview homeowners who share our appreciation for healthy, well-managed trees.
